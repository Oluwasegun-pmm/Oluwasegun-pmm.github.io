Product Thesis

AI earns its place in the enterprise when it's measurably trustworthy.

Most AI products die at the compliance review, not the demo. The winning GTM posture across Core AI, Data, and Cloud-Native platforms is to message auditability, explainability, and measurable reliability from day one — not bolt a trust story on after a pilot.

I build GTM the same way. Every AI launch I've led at Microsoft — from M365 Copilot quality benchmarks to Arc SQL customer evidence pipelines — starts with a written evaluation narrative, a clear proof point, and a metric that can falsify the pitch.

That discipline is why a 100+ engagement customer evidence pipeline could cut TEI sourcing time 90%, and why a written evaluation framework is what gets a stalled AI pilot past risk & compliance — not another capability demo. It's the same posture, run at every scale, in domains where the cost of a false positive is measured in lost deals and broken production — not Slack apologies.
