Customer Story

Factorial scales HR automation across Europe and Latin America on Azure Kubernetes Service

Migrating off a competing cloud to AKS and Azure OpenAI let Factorial's engineers stop managing infrastructure and start building AI agents that eliminate the busywork managers were drowning in.

Customer: Factorial Industry: HR / People Management Software Product: Azure Kubernetes Service

01The challenge

Managers at the small and medium-sized businesses Factorial serves were buried in manual administrative work — absence tracking, document management, vacation approvals — that left no time for the part of HR that actually retains people: culture and development. As one customer put it, HR teams were "overwhelmed with operational tasks" that diverted attention from strategic initiatives.

02The solution

Factorial migrated from a competing cloud provider to Microsoft Azure to get AI infrastructure, interoperability with the business applications its customers already run, and a platform that could scale go-to-market globally. The stack:

  • Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) — with real-time autoscaling for onboarding surges across Italy, France, Portugal, and Latin America
  • Azure Database for MySQL — cut operational overhead so engineers could spend time on features, not database maintenance
  • Azure OpenAI Service with Foundry Models — powering AI agents that answer employee questions and trigger workflows autonomously
  • Microsoft Teams, Outlook, and Dynamics 365 Business Central — the surfaces those AI agents plug into for a seamless employee experience

03The results

65%
Reduction in administrative time
2 days

34%

Reduction in involuntary turnover

The AI agents didn't just automate tasks — they gave managers back the time and attention that drives retention, which is the actual metric HR leaders are judged on.

"AI is not here to take jobs but to eliminate boring tasks. This technology frees us to focus on meaningful work." Carlos Cuadrado Barril, Managing Director, h&k
