01The problem

Azure Arc-Enabled SQL Server had extraordinary technical capability and a massive business target, but no engine for producing proof. TEI (Total Economic Impact) evidence took weeks to source for every deal, field teams re-built customer stories from scratch, and engineering lacked a synthesized view of what enterprise customers actually wanted next.

02The insight

The product team was treating customer evidence as a marketing deliverable. In reality it was a product function: a pipeline that connected field engagements → standardized story artifacts → VoC signal → roadmap priorities. Build the pipeline, and evidence stops being the bottleneck — it becomes the accelerator.

03Decisions I made

  • Built the customer evidence pipeline from zero — nominated, qualified, and standardized stories across 100+ engagements.
  • Instrumented sourcing so TEI and customer proof artifacts were reusable across marketing, field, and engineering.
  • Synthesized Voice of Customer and Voice of Field from those 100+ engagements into prioritized insights that directly fed engineering priorities and C-suite GTM decisions.
  • Formally documented AI output quality standards and monitoring requirements for M365 Copilot — ensuring compliance, explainability, and auditability in enterprise deployments.

04Outcome

  • TEI sourcing time reduced by 90%, unblocking deal velocity.
  • Delivered the World Bank flagship customer story at Microsoft Ignite — anchoring the keynote narrative for hybrid public sector. Read the full customer story →
  • Contributed to scaling Arc-Enabled SQL Server as a $10Bn product business.
  • VoC → roadmap synthesis shaped engineering prioritization beyond a single fiscal year.

05Learnings

  • Evidence is infrastructure. If the team has to improvise proof each quarter, the product is leaving money on the table.
  • VoC without synthesis is noise. 100 interviews are worthless without the PMM pattern-matching artifact.
  • Regulated AI benchmarks compound. The M365 Copilot quality standards authored here became the seed of the AI evaluation framework now leading across Core AI, Data, and Cloud-Native platforms.
