AKS Partner Showcase & Forum — rebuilding a partner motion

Stood up the first AKS Partner Forum (400+ partners, C-level speakers), signed 4 strategic partners in a Showcase series, and re-engaged 50+ disengaged OSS MVPs to reverse a community trust deficit.

01The problem

AKS had a partner motion degrading on two fronts simultaneously: strategic partners lacked a repeatable showcase and co-sell path, and the OSS & MVP community was voting with its feet — trust was eroding and senior OSS MVPs were openly disengaging from Microsoft-owned channels. Without both, AKS couldn't credibly claim to be the platform for cloud-native AI.

02The insight

Partner-led pipeline isn't an events problem — it's a product storytelling infrastructure problem. What was missing was a repeatable showcase format with clear commit gates, combined with a community re-engagement arc that treated MVPs as signal, not broadcast targets.

03Decisions I made

  • Launched the AKS Partner Showcase Series — a repeatable format with commit gates that secured 4 strategic partners into co-sell motions.
  • Founded the first AKS Partner Forum, attracting 400+ partners with C-level speakers — purpose-built as repeatable GTM infrastructure for partner-led pipeline.
  • Targeted 50+ disengaged OSS MVPs at MVP Summit with AKS-specific positioning addressing their stated friction points.
  • Positioned AKS as the platform for agentic AI workloads — containerized inferencing, secure model deployment, intelligent orchestration — giving partners a coherent pitch.

04Outcome

  • 4 strategic partners secured through the Showcase.
  • 400+ partner forum attendees with a C-level speaker lineup, establishing a recurring GTM asset.
  • OSS community trust visibly rebuilt — 50+ disengaged MVPs re-engaged with renewed advocacy beyond Microsoft-owned channels.
  • Agentic workload positioning adopted by field and partner motion.

05Learnings

  • Community trust is earned by naming what went wrong. Re-engagement worked because the first conversation wasn't a pitch.
  • Events scale when they're products. The Forum had acceptance criteria, a roadmap, and a retro — just like any feature.
  • Strategic partners buy clarity before capability. "The platform for agentic AI workloads" travels further than feature bullets.
